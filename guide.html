How to use Namaz Tracker

A short guide to every screen and tool, with the exact buttons to tap. Each card shows whether it is Free or needs Pro. Pro can be tried free for 7 days.

Getting started

Install and set up

Three things to do once: install the app, tell it where you are, and pick your language.

Free

Install the app

Namaz Tracker is installed from an APK file, so Android will ask for one permission.

How to use

  1. Tap Download for Android on this site and wait for the file (about 48 MB).
  2. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
  3. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source (your browser), then tap Install again.
  4. Open Namaz Tracker and allow notifications when it asks.
Free

Set your location

Prayer times are worked out on your phone for the place you choose.

How to use

  1. On first launch tap Set location. Later, open Settings and tap your location.
  2. Tap Use my current location for GPS, or type your city under or search a city and pick it from the list.
  3. Optional: turn on Update automatically when travelling so times refresh when you move.
Free

Match your masjid's times

If your masjid's times differ a little, move any prayer earlier or later.

How to use

  1. Open Settings and tap Adjust times manually.
  2. Pick a prayer and set the minutes (plus or minus) until it matches your masjid.
Free

Choose your language

The whole app works in English, हिन्दी and اردو.

How to use

  1. Open Settings.
  2. At the top, tap English, हिन्दी or اردو. The change is immediate.
Daily tracking

Mark prayers and make up qaza

Open the app after each prayer. It takes a second, and your record stays honest.

Free

Mark your prayers

The Today tab lists the five prayers with their times.

How to use

  1. Open the Today tab.
  2. Tap ✓ Prayed when you have prayed, or ✗ Missed if you haven't. Tap it again to undo.
  3. Tap a prayer's row for more choices: Prayed, In jama'at, Prayed late, Missed or Clear.
  4. Before Fajr, Last night's Isha shows at the top so you can still mark it.
Free

Catch up on unmarked prayers

If you forgot to mark a few, the Today tab tells you.

How to use

  1. Tap the notice Earlier prayers not marked.
  2. Mark each one ✓ or ✗, or tap All prayed and confirm to mark them all at once.
Free

Qaza list

A prayer marked missed goes to your qaza list, with its date.

How to use

  1. Mark a prayer ✗ Missed. It is added to qaza.
  2. Open the Qaza tab. To make up lists missed prayers, oldest first.
  3. After you pray the qaza, tap ✓ next to it. Tap Undo if you slipped.
  4. The Made up tab shows your total and this month's count.
Free

Add older qaza in bulk

For prayers you missed before you started using the app.

How to use

  1. In the Qaza tab, tap the Add older qaza icon at the top right.
  2. Enter Years and Months and tap Fill, or set a number for each prayer.
  3. Tap Add. They appear under Older missed prayers; tap Prayed 1 each time you make one up.
Free + Pro

History and stats

A calendar of your days, with streaks and a bar for each prayer.

How to use

  1. Open the History tab.
  2. Switch between 7d and 30d to change the range.
  3. Tap any day on the calendar to see it or fix it.

The last 30 days are free. The 1y view and older days need Pro.

Reminders

Alerts that fit your day

Everything here is in Settings → Reminders, ringtones & sounds.

Free

Prayer alerts

Each prayer has its own alert, sound and on/off setting.

How to use

  1. Open Settings → Reminders, ringtones & sounds.
  2. Under PRAYER ALERTS, tap a prayer and choose its sound, or turn it off.
  3. Tap Send a test alert to hear it.
Pro

Your own adhan or ringtone

Use any ringtone on your phone, or upload an adhan MP3.

How to use

  1. Tap a prayer, or the ♪ button next to it.
  2. Tap Choose ringtone / sound… for a sound already on your phone, or Upload your own sound… for an audio file (up to 10 MB).
  3. Tap Use this sound for all prayers if you want the same one every time.
Free

Reminder before prayer

A heads-up a few minutes before each prayer.

How to use

  1. Turn on Remind me before prayer.
  2. Choose how many minutes before you want it.
Free

“Did you pray?” check

If a prayer is still unmarked near the end of its time, the app asks.

How to use

  1. Turn on “Did you pray?” check.
  2. When the notification comes, tap ✓ Prayed or ✗ Missed. You don't need to open the app.
  3. Not yet? Tap Remind in 15 min.
Free

Daily qaza reminder

A nudge once a day while you still have qaza to make up.

How to use

  1. Turn on Daily qaza reminder.
  2. Tap Reminder time and pick the time that suits you.
Pro

Strong Fajr alarm

Keeps ringing until you are really awake.

How to use

  1. Open Reminders, ringtones & sounds.
  2. Turn on Strong Fajr alarm.
  3. Allow exact alarms when Android asks (see the tip below).
Pro

Auto-silent during namaz

Your phone goes quiet 2 minutes after each adhan and returns to normal afterwards.

How to use

  1. Turn on Auto-silent during namaz.
  2. Android needs “Do Not Disturb access”: tap Open settings, find Namaz Tracker, turn it on, then come back.
  3. Choose Keep quiet for how many minutes, and Vibrate or Silent.
Pro

Alarm mode

Plays prayer alerts on the alarm volume, even when the phone is on silent.

How to use

  1. Under HOW ALERTS SOUND, turn on Alarm mode.
  2. Use the Vibrate switch beside it to add vibration.

Make sure reminders arrive

Under RELIABILITY on the same screen, tap Allow notifications and allow exact alarms (Android calls it “Alarms & reminders”). Then tap Disable battery optimisation. Some phones, such as Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo and Samsung, block alarms otherwise.

Tools

Extra help for your worship

Open the Tools tab. Everything here is Pro, except joining an accountability partner. You can try it all free for 7 days.

Pro

Qaza planner

Turns your pending qaza into a daily goal and a finish date.

How to use

  1. Open Tools → Qaza planner.
  2. Set your Daily goal, the number of qaza you will make up each day.
  3. See the date you'll be done and today's progress.
Pro

Digital tasbeeh

A counter you tap, with today's count and your total.

How to use

  1. Open Tools → Digital tasbeeh.
  2. Choose 33, 99, 100 or no limit (∞). With 33 you count 33, 33 and 34, which makes 100.
  3. Tap anywhere on the screen to count. Reset starts over.
Pro

Azkar & daily duas

A checklist for the morning, the evening and after salah.

How to use

  1. Open Tools → Azkar & daily duas.
  2. Choose Morning, Evening or After salah.
  3. Tick each one as you read it. The top shows how many are done.
Pro

Qibla compass & nearby masjids

Shows the direction of Makkah and finds masjids around you.

How to use

  1. Open Tools → Qibla compass & nearby masjids. Your location must be set.
  2. Hold your phone flat. The mosque icon points to the Qibla; turn until it is at the top.
  3. No compass sensor? Point the top of the phone north instead. If the reading seems stuck, move the phone in a figure 8 to calibrate.
  4. Tap Find nearby masjids to see them on a map.
Pro

Masjid jama'at times

Save your masjid's jama'at times and get a reminder before each.

How to use

  1. Open Tools → Masjid jama'at times.
  2. Tap a prayer and set its jama'at time. The adhan time is shown for reference.
  3. Turn on Remind me before jama'at and choose how many minutes.
Pro

Islamic calendar & events

Today's Hijri date, with Ramadan, the Eids and other key dates.

How to use

  1. Open Tools → Islamic calendar & events.
  2. Dates follow local moon sighting. In India and Pakistan the month usually starts a day after Saudi Arabia.
  3. If your date is off by a day, go to Settings → Hijri date correction and adjust it.
Pro

Smart insights & badges

Shows which prayer is improving, which needs care, and what you've earned.

How to use

  1. Open Tools → Smart insights for your strongest and weakest prayer and your jama'at share.
  2. Open Tools → Badges & achievements for streak and qaza milestones.
  3. Streak protection forgives one missed day a month.
Pro

Monthly PDF report

This month's record as a PDF you can save or share.

How to use

  1. Open Tools → Smart insights.
  2. Tap Share this month as PDF and choose where to send it.
Pro

Family mode

Mark your children's or family's prayers on your own phone.

How to use

  1. Open Tools → Family mode and tap Add member. You can add up to 8 people.
  2. Type a name, such as Aisha.
  3. Each day, tap ✓ or ✗ on their prayers. They don't need an account.
Free to join

Accountability partner

Link with a friend or spouse to keep each other going.

How to use

  1. Log in, then open Tools → Accountability partner.
  2. Tap Invite a partner. You get a code that works once and lasts 48 hours. Send it to the person you choose.
  3. They enter it under Got a code from someone? and tap Join.

Partners see only your name, your streak and how many prayers you prayed today, never which ones you missed. Creating an invite needs Pro.

Pro

Travel mode (Qasr)

Reminds you about shortened prayers when you are far from home.

How to use

  1. Keep Update automatically when travelling on in Settings.
  2. About 80 km or more from home, a Travelling banner appears on the Today tab.
  3. Tap Set this place as home on the banner if you have moved for good.

Please follow your own scholar's guidance on Qasr.

Pro

Ramadan mode

Sehri and Iftar countdown, fasting and Taraweeh.

How to use

  1. It turns on by itself in Ramadan. To use it at other times, open Tools and switch on Ramadan mode.
  2. The Today tab then shows Sehri ends and Iftar.
Pro

Extra worship tracking

Sunnah, Witr, Tahajjud, fasting and Quran pages.

How to use

  1. On the Today tab, find More worship today.
  2. Tap Sunnah, Witr, Tahajjud or Fasting to tick it. Taraweeh appears in Ramadan.
  3. Use – and + to count the Quran pages you read.
Pro

App colour themes

Change the colour of the whole app.

How to use

  1. Open Settings → App colour.
  2. Tap a colour.
Widgets

Check prayers without opening the app

Five home-screen widgets are available. Two are free.

Free

Add a widget

Put a widget on your home screen.

How to use

  1. Open Settings and scroll to HOME SCREEN WIDGETS.
  2. Tap a widget, then Try adding automatically. Or press and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets and find Namaz Tracker.
  3. On Today's tracker, tap a prayer to mark it ✓.

The five widgets

Free widgets work right away. The others show a lock until you have Pro.

  • Next prayerCountdown to the next prayerFree
  • Today's trackerTap a prayer to mark it ✓Free
  • Prayer timesAll of today's timesPro
  • Qaza counterPrayers left to make upPro
  • All-in-oneNext prayer, tracker and qazaPro
Account & backup

Keep your record safe

An account is optional. You only need one for Pro, cloud backup and accountability partners.

Free

Log in or create an account

Log in with Google or with your email.

How to use

  1. Open Settings and tap Account at the top.
  2. Tap Continue with Google, or enter your email and tap Log in. New here? Tap Create account.
  3. Open the email we send you and tap the link to confirm, then log in.

Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? on the same screen. Logging out keeps your prayers on the phone.

Pro

Cloud backup and restore

Your record is saved online, so a lost or new phone isn't a loss.

How to use

  1. Log in. Pro backs up automatically after every change. You can also tap Back up now.
  2. On a new phone, log in, open Settings and tap your name at the top (your account).
  3. Tap Restore from cloud and confirm.
Free

Back up to a file

A backup file you keep yourself. No account needed.

How to use

  1. Open Settings, find YOUR DATA and tap Back up history.
  2. Save the file or send it to yourself (Drive, WhatsApp, email).
  3. On a new phone, move the file across, tap Restore from backup and choose it.
Pro & payment

Pay once, nothing renews

Pro is ₹99 for 30 days or ₹699 for 365 days. One payment, no automatic renewal.

Free

Start the free trial

Every account gets one 7-day trial of every Pro feature.

How to use

  1. Log in, then open Settings and tap Go Pro.
  2. Tap Start 7-day free trial. No payment is needed.
Free

Buy Pro

Pay by UPI, card or wallet through Cashfree.

How to use

  1. Open Settings → Go Pro and choose 30 days or 365 days.
  2. Enter your mobile number (the payment company needs it for your receipt) and tap the pay button (for example Pay ₹99 now).
  3. Finish the payment on the page that opens, then return to the app. Pro turns on within a minute.

Paying while Pro is active adds the new period to the end of the current one.

How to use

  1. Open Settings and tap your name at the top.
  2. Tap Refresh my plan.
  3. Still nothing? Email help@fableora.com with the payment date and amount.
