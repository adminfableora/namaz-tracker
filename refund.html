Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Cancellation

Pro is a one-time payment for a fixed period (1 month or 1 year) and does not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. Pro simply ends when the period is over.

Refunds

  • If you were charged but Pro did not activate, contact us with your payment details and we will activate Pro or refund you in full.
  • If you were charged twice for the same purchase, we refund the extra payment in full.
  • You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of payment if you are not satisfied.

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

How to ask

Email help@fableora.com with your account email and the date and amount of the payment.
