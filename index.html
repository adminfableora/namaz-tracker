Help

Questions people ask

Is Namaz Tracker free?

Yes. Prayer times, reminders, tracking, the qaza list, 30 days of history and two widgets are free, with no ads. Pro is optional.

How are the prayer times calculated?

On your phone, from your location, using the calculation method commonly followed in your region. If your masjid's times differ a little, you can move any prayer a few minutes earlier or later in Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. You only need one for Pro, including the free trial, cloud backup and accountability partners. You can log in with Google or with your email.

Who can see my prayers?

Only you. Without an account, your record never leaves your phone, and a Pro cloud backup can be read only by you. If you link with an accountability partner, they see only your name, your current streak and how many prayers you prayed today — never which ones you missed.

How does paying for Pro work?

Inside the app, tap Go Pro and choose 30 days or 365 days. You pay once by UPI, card or wallet through Cashfree, and Pro turns on within a minute. It never renews by itself.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you're not happy, email us within 7 days of paying and we'll refund you in full. Details are in our refund policy.

I changed phones. Can I keep my history?

Yes. With Pro, log in on the new phone and restore from the cloud. On the free plan, use Settings → Back up history, move the file to your new phone and choose Restore from backup.
